About me

Hi, I’m Kailyu Ye, an undergraduate student in Statistics at Zhejiang University. I study in the School of Mathematical Sciences and expect to graduate in June 2028.

My research interests are high-dimensional statistics, machine learning, statistical inference, and large language models. I am currently studying online selective classification with expert review under Prof. Wenguang Sun. I also work on dynamic-evolution mixture-of-experts vision-language-action models with Prof. Zemin Liu.

Previously, I completed an academic reading project on An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Prof. Lijun Wang. My Research page includes these projects and reports from my multivariate statistical analysis course.

Outside research and coursework, I serve as a student ambassador for the School of Mathematical Sciences and as Youth League Branch Secretary for Statistics 2401.
