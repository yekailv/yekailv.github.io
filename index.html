Hi, I’m Kailyu, an undergraduate student in Statistics at Zhejiang University.

I study in the School of Mathematical Sciences and expect to graduate in June 2028. My research interests lie in high-dimensional statistics, machine learning, statistical inference, and large language models.

I am currently working on a Student Research Training Program with Prof. Zemin Liu, exploring dynamic-evolution mixture-of-experts vision-language-action models for embodied intelligence. Previously, I completed an academic reading project on An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Prof. Lijun Wang.

Beyond coursework and research, I serve as a student ambassador for the School of Mathematical Sciences and as Youth League Branch Secretary for Statistics 2401.
